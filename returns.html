Returns & refund policy

30-day returns

You may request a return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original condition and packaging.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

Contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos. We'll send a replacement or a full refund — you won't pay return shipping.

Change of mind

Return shipping is paid by the customer. We'll provide the return address after approval (do not send items without contacting us).

EU/UK customers

You have a statutory right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. This policy does not limit your legal rights.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive and inspect the return (or approve a damage claim).

Non-returnable

Hygiene items once opened, and items damaged by misuse.
