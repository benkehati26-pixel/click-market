Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed in 1–3 business days. Delivery typically takes 7–20 business days depending on destination. See the shipping policy.

How much is shipping?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $49, otherwise $4.99 — one rule for every destination. Actual carrier rates depend on our supplier; if a destination costs more, we'll tell you before you pay.

Where do you ship?

We plan to ship to most countries worldwide. If we can't ship to your address, we will tell you before you pay.

Do I pay customs or import duties?

Depending on your country and order value, customs duties or VAT may be charged by your local authorities. These are the buyer's responsibility unless stated otherwise at checkout.

Which currency am I charged in?

Orders are charged in USD. EUR, GBP and ILS prices are shown at fixed illustrative rates for convenience; your bank's rate may differ.

Can I return an item?

Yes — within 30 days of delivery, subject to the conditions in our returns policy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Payments are not enabled yet (pre-launch). We plan to accept PayPal, including cards through PayPal.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can also use our Track order page.

Why are there no reviews?

We're a new store. We only show reviews from verified buyers and will never post fake reviews.

How do I contact you?

Use the contact page. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days.
